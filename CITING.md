# How to cite IDROCK

**IDROCK AI Lab, New Uzbekistan University, Tashkent, Uzbekistan**

This page tells you how to acknowledge our GPU compute, cite our lab, and cite
our software and datasets. Questions: [idrock@newuu.uz](mailto:idrock@newuu.uz).

---

## 1. Acknowledging GPU compute

If you used IDROCK's GPU computing resources, please include the following in
the acknowledgments section of your paper:

> This research used the GPU computing resources of the IDROCK AI Lab at New
> Uzbekistan University (Tashkent, Uzbekistan), provided under IDROCK compute
> allocation **[PROJECT-ID]**.

Short version (posters, blog posts, talks):

> Research supported with GPU compute from the IDROCK AI Lab, New Uzbekistan
> University.

Replace `[PROJECT-ID]` with the allocation ID you received with your grant
(e.g. `IDROCK-2026-014`). If you don't have one, ask us.

**Please also email us a link to every accepted paper, preprint, or thesis that
used our resources** ([idrock@newuu.uz](mailto:idrock@newuu.uz)). We use this to
report the cluster's impact and to keep providing free compute.

## 2. Citing the lab

To cite the IDROCK AI Lab or its GPU cluster directly:

```bibtex
@misc{idrock2026cluster,
  author       = {{IDROCK AI Lab}},
  title        = {{IDROCK} {GPU} Computing Cluster},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://idrock.uz}},
  note         = {IDROCK AI Lab, New Uzbekistan University, Tashkent, Uzbekistan}
}
```

## 3. Citing our software and datasets

Each of our repositories carries its own citation:

- If the repo has a **`CITATION.cff`** file, use GitHub's **"Cite this
  repository"** button (right sidebar) to export APA or BibTeX.
- Otherwise, use the BibTeX block in the repo's **`## Citation`** section.
- Datasets: cite the DOI given on the dataset card (e.g. the DTM benchmark,
  DOI [10.21227/e4h4-kp42](https://doi.org/10.21227/e4h4-kp42)).

## 4. For lab members: affiliation in papers

Use this exact affiliation string in every byline, arXiv submission, and
profile — consistency is what makes the lab's output countable:

> **IDROCK AI Lab, New Uzbekistan University, Tashkent, Uzbekistan**

(Short form where space is tight: *IDROCK AI Lab, New Uzbekistan University*.)

Also:

- **ORCID** — add New Uzbekistan University as your organization via the
  registry picker (ROR [`035v3tr79`](https://ror.org/035v3tr79)); don't
  free-type the name.
- **Google Scholar** — verify your profile with your `@newuu.uz` email and
  enter the affiliation string above verbatim.
- **OpenReview** — register with your `@newuu.uz` email and select
  New Uzbekistan University as your institution.
