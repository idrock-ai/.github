# Code of Conduct

The IDROCK AI Lab is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone who participates in our projects and community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional toward others.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Insulting, derogatory, or trolling comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all IDROCK project spaces — repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions — and when representing the lab in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report violations to [idrock@newuu.uz](mailto:idrock@newuu.uz). Reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content or
restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.
